My path here wasn’t linear. I started in graphic design, where I developed an appreciation for visual storytelling, composition, and the quiet details that make an experience feel right.

Curiosity pulled me from static layouts into UX/UI and fullstack frontend engineering. Over years of building production web applications, I never saw the interface as mere decoration layered over backend plumbing—to me, the interface and the intelligence behind it work together as one human experience.

Today, my work centers on AI engineering. Most AI tools expose raw model complexity—endless prompt boxes, unpredictable latency, and opaque outputs. I treat intelligence as an expressive design material: combining agentic workflows, structured reasoning, and tactile frontend craft so complex capabilities feel calm, transparent, and effortless.

I'm interested in the bigger picture: how things work, why they work that way, how people interact with them, and how they can be made better.

I enjoy bringing ideas to life across the entire process, combining design thinking, user experience, and engineering to create digital experiences that are thoughtful, intuitive, and human.

How I Think

Four ways of seeing.

I do my best work where disciplines overlap—connecting the nuance of a micro-interaction to the architecture of an intelligent system.

01 — DESIGN

Make it meaningful.

Visual language, editorial hierarchy, and tactile craft. Design isn’t surface polish—it’s how a product earns trust and communicates clarity at a glance.

02 — EXPERIENCE

Make it make sense.

Understanding human mental models, reducing cognitive load, and turning ambiguous workflows into calm, intuitive journeys people actually enjoy using.

03 — ENGINEERING

Make it real.

Fullstack and frontend architecture built with rigor—accessible, resilient, fast, and engineered so design intent survives intact all the way to production.

04 — INTELLIGENCE

Make it adaptive.

Applied AI systems, agentic workflows, and streaming interfaces designed around human agency—where machine intelligence feels effortless, not intimidating.

Let’s work together / 05

Let’s make something transformative.

Bring me an ambiguous product problem, an AI workflow that needs human clarity, or a 0-to-1 idea ready to be designed and built end-to-end.
